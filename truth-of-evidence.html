03

Reality does not change when it is misunderstood. Evidence is the light that remains after certainty fractures.

The third truth
Reality survives our refusal

The Earth moved while we placed ourselves at its centre. Microbes spread while we blamed poisoned air. The world does not wait for human permission to be what it already is.

Ten collisions
with reality.

These are not stories of science defeating foolish people. They are warnings about how authority, identity, profit and habit can become walls between observation and acceptance.

The evidence does not match that search. Try another word.

Carry the truth with you

Reality is not
a vote.

The evidence may humiliate an old belief, expose an institution or dismantle a comfortable story. That discomfort does not weaken the truth. It marks the exact place where understanding has been asked to grow.
