THE VERY SERIOUS ADMISSIONS OFFICE

Three things required.

Curiosity does not belong to one age, one country, one curriculum or one kind of learner.

01

Be willing to learn.

Especially when the first explanation makes absolutely no sense.

02

Be willing to explore.

You do not need to know where a question is going before you follow it.

03

Be willing to be wrong.

A mistake is information for the next attempt, not a permanent label.

NOT REQUIRED: perfect grades already knowing looking clever one “correct” path
