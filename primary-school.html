FIELD NOTE 01

Learning used to hide inside ordinary life.

Mathematics lived in markets and games. Science lived in weather, soil and cooking. Language travelled through songs and stories. Design began with a useful problem. This trail brings those habits back—carefully adapted for children today.

“Watch. Wonder. Make. Move. Explain.”

FIELD NOTE 02

Our promise

Traditions are not costumes.

These activities are inspired by real ways people have learned across time. Living cultural knowledge deserves specific names, accurate sources and community voices. Sacred stories, symbols and practices should never be copied as classroom decoration.
