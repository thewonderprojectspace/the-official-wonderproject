The grown-up corner

Help without quietly doing it for them.

Children need time to think aloud, change direction and be temporarily wrong. Your questions can protect that time.

Instead ofThat’s wrong. Try again.→TryShow me the part that made sense first.
Instead ofThis is easy!→TryWhich part feels familiar, and which part feels new?
Instead ofHow don’t you know this?→TryWhat could help us find out?
Instead ofLet me show you the right way.→TryCan you show me your way before we compare another one?

Curriculum clarity

02✦

Thinking value

The activity develops understanding—not only busy hands or completed boxes.

03◎

Human instructions

Children and adults know what to do, why it matters and when to pause.

Accessible dignity
