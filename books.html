Learning does not have to feel cold, quiet, or overly serious. This is a colourful library for children, teenagers, educators, university students, professionals and curious adults who still want to ask questions, make things and enjoy discovering something new. Learning can be joyful and meaningful

Serious learning. Playful spirit.

The Wonder Project is designed for people who want to grow without losing their spark. A student can practise fractions, a teacher can discover a new classroom idea, a medical student can revise science, and an adult can read something simply because it makes them wonder.
