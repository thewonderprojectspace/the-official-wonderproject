Open call · original voices only

Please send us your story.

We love original, authentic art and the stories of a life that changed you, questioned you or quietly made you who you are. Share a short true story, reflection, blog or poem—in any language.

  • You may use your real name or a pen name.
  • Your work must be your own.
  • Nothing is published automatically. We will contact you first.
  • Please do not include private details about other people without their permission.
