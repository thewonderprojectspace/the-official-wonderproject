Our slightly uncomfortable idea

The purpose of learning is not to be given the truth. It is to become brave, patient and honest enough to seek it.

We live in a world where answers arrive before questions have had time to become interesting. Here, technology may point to a doorway—but original resources, books, practice, people and lived experience help you walk through it.

So leave the couch occasionally. Ask a human. Open the whole book. Make something with your hands. Test the idea. Change your mind. The goal is not one universal truth; it is a more honest relationship with your own learning.

The source room

Search engines can locate. Humans still have to understand.

Jigyasa Verse should guide learners towards stronger evidence, deeper conversations and first-hand experience—not manufacture certainty where it does not exist.

Ask: Who created this—and why?
Trace: Can I reach the original book, study, person or record?
Test: What happens when this idea meets evidence or experience?
Reflect: Am I being honest about what I know, assume and still need to learn?
