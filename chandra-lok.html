Eight phases. No hierarchy.

The lunar cycle is not a staircase. A Full Moon is not more complete than a New Moon. Choose the phase that resembles where you are—not where you think you should be.

Phase 01 · New Moon

The Unseen Self

Who are you when nobody is looking?

Some parts of us exist long before we can explain them. This phase is for hidden dreams, private grief, silence, loneliness and the versions of ourselves that have not yet found language.

  • Feeling invisible
  • Masking emotions
  • Identity and private becoming
  • The truth behind “I’m fine”
Direct link to this phase
Phase 02 · Waxing Crescent

The First Attempt

What if courage begins before confidence?

Not every beginning looks brave. Sometimes it is answering one message, asking for help, returning after failure or trying again while a part of you still expects to fall.

  • Starting again
  • Fragile hope
  • First boundaries
  • Learning badly before learning well
Direct link to this phase
Phase 03 · First Quarter

The Resistance

What do you protect when the world asks you to abandon it?

Becoming creates friction. This phase holds criticism, rejection, family expectations, peer pressure, misunderstood choices and the difficult work of saying no without losing yourself.

  • Conflict and opposition
  • Pressure to conform
  • Holding a boundary
  • Choosing an unfamiliar path
Direct link to this phase
Phase 04 · Waxing Gibbous

The Weight of Becoming

Can progress be real before it becomes visible?

This is the exhausting middle: too far from the beginning to feel new, but too far from the result to feel certain. It holds comparison, invisible effort and the pressure to prove that your work matters.

  • Burnout and perfectionism
  • Feeling behind
  • Invisible discipline
  • Carrying several dreams
Direct link to this phase
Phase 05 · Full Moon

The Seen Self

What changes when the world begins to notice you?

Visibility can bring joy, connection and recognition. It can also create pressure to remain impressive. A Full Moon is completely visible, but visibility is not the same as being understood.

  • Achievement and recognition
  • Confidence and belonging
  • Body image
  • Fear of losing success
Direct link to this phase
Phase 06 · Waning Gibbous

The Reflection

What did the experience reveal about you?

Reflection begins after the loudest moment has passed. Here we make space for memory, gratitude, regret, responsibility and the difficult discovery that we may also have caused harm.

  • Accountability
  • Changing your mind
  • Lessons after success
  • Forgiveness without entitlement
Direct link to this phase
Phase 07 · Last Quarter

The Letting Go

What remains when an old version of your life ends?

Some endings are chosen and some arrive without permission. This phase holds grief, changing relationships, leaving familiar places and accepting that not everything can be repaired.

  • Grief and endings
  • Outgrowing an identity
  • Relationships that change
  • Releasing expectations
Direct link to this phase
Phase 08 · Waning Crescent

The Quiet Return

Can you rest without believing you have disappeared?

The quiet is not emptiness. This phase is for rest, recovery, solitude and the slow preparation for another beginning. The cycle does not restart because you failed; it continues because you are alive.

  • Rest without guilt
  • Healing and solitude
  • Rebuilding trust
  • Beginning again
Direct link to this phase

The Craters — What Leaves a Mark

A crater is not the whole Moon. It is evidence that something happened. This space examines harm without turning suffering into an identity or treating it as a necessary phase of growth.
