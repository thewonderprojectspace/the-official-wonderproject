Chandra Lok

You are not one permanent version of yourself. You appear, disappear, begin, resist, become, break, reflect and return.

Like the Moon, you may carry marks. Like the Moon, you remain whole.

Eight phases.
No hierarchy.

A Full Moon is not better than a New Moon. Visibility is not always progress, and darkness is not always failure. Choose the phase that feels closest to where you are now.

What phases alone cannot hold.

Some experiences do not belong to one moment in the cycle. They can appear at any time, shape several phases and leave lasting marks.
