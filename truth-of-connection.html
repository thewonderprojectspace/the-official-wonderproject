04

Nothing living exists entirely alone. Life blooms because another life was already there —feeding, carrying, protecting, transforming or returning what was lost.

The fourth truth
Life is made between lives

A flower contains sunlight, soil, rain, fungi, bacteria and the memory of every creature that returned itself to the earth. What appears individual is often a meeting disguised as one body.

The grammar of a living world

Nothing ends.
It enters another life.

Energy arrives, plants hold it, animals move it, decomposers release it and soil makes it available again. The cycle is not a perfect circle—it is millions of exchanges holding one another open.

Ten records
of interdependence.

From fungal threads beneath forests to bacteria inside bodies, these records reveal that independence is often an illusion created by looking at life too closely.

Carry the truth with you

You were never
growing alone.

Somebody held the door, carried the pollen, fed the soil, challenged the thought, named the wound or stayed long enough for darkness to become survivable. Connection does not make a life less its own. It reveals how much life was required for that life to bloom.
