The Change

02

Change is not the opposite of truth. It is the consequence of truth acting through time, pressure and possibility.

The second truth
Nothing living remains still

Stone enters the furnace as one thing and leaves carrying another form. Change does not betray what was true. It reveals what truth can become.

Ten records
of becoming.

From mutations inside a single cell to extinctions that reshape the planet, these records follow the forces that destroy old forms—and make new ones possible.

No form emerged from that search. Try another word.

Carry the truth with you

You are allowed
to become unrecognisable.

Molten stone does not apologise for losing its first shape. Mutation does not ask the past for permission. What you become will not erase what you were—it will prove that you remained in contact with reality long enough to be changed by it.
