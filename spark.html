The part nobody puts on a poster Motivation is unreliable

Do not build the plan around a feeling that keeps changing.

Motivation may introduce you to a goal. It rarely stays for the whole project. Build a tiny rhythm that still works when you are bored, busy, uncertain or not feeling particularly inspirational.

Your mood can say “not today.” Your decision can answer, “five minutes.”
