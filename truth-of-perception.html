We do not experience all of reality. We experience what the mind can construct from it —using fragments, predictions, memory and attention.

The sixth truth
Experience is an interpretation

The world reaches the senses as light, pressure, chemicals and vibration. Somewhere between signal and awareness, the mind creates colour, meaning, continuity and self. Perception is not a window. It is a living construction.

A quiet experiment

The context changes.
The colour does not.

The two centre circles use exactly the same indigo. Their surroundings make one appear lighter and the other darker. Perception measures relationships—not isolated values.
