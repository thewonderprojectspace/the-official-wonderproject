What Are You Curious About?

Some answers are scientific, some are surprising, and some may lead to even more questions.

The answer

Sunlight contains many colours. When sunlight enters Earth's atmosphere, tiny particles scatter blue light more strongly than most other colours. That scattered blue light reaches our eyes from across the sky.

Keep wondering: Why does the sky sometimes become orange, pink, or red?

The answer

Many animals experience sleep stages similar to humans, including rapid eye movement sleep. Scientists have observed behaviours suggesting that animals such as dogs, cats, rats, and birds may dream.

Keep wondering: What might your pet be dreaming about?

💭

The answer

During sleep, the brain does not always store dreams in the same way it stores waking memories. Unless we wake during or soon after a dream and think about it, the memory can disappear very quickly.

Keep wondering: Could writing dreams down help us remember them?

The answer

Plants do not recognise others in the same way humans do, but they can respond to chemical signals, light, touch, competition, and nearby organisms. Some plants behave differently around genetically related plants.

Keep wondering: Can plants warn one another about danger?

Sound needs matter, such as air, to travel. Space has almost no matter, so ordinary sound cannot move through it. Astronauts communicate using radios, which send electromagnetic waves that can travel through space.
