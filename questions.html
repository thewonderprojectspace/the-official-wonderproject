03 / WHERE DID IT BEGIN?

Your past can explain the calling.
It does not have to control it.

An experience, regret, loss or mistake can become something useful. The question is whether you are choosing the lesson—or still being chased by the wound.

THENSomething happened.

You struggled, failed, stayed too long, trusted the wrong person, or became someone you no longer recognised.

NOWYou understand more.

Your experience is yours. You decide what it teaches you and which patterns end with you.

NEXTIt may help someone else.

Not because pain makes you special—but because what you learned may make another person's road less lonely.

05 / YOUR LIFE IS ALSO A CHOICE

Not everything deserves continued access to you.

You decide when a habit ends, when a lifestyle no longer feels healthy, and when distance from a person becomes necessary. Be gentle with yourself—but firm about what repeatedly pulls you away from who you are trying to become.
