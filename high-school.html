The subject multiverse

Pick a world. Get curious.

Each subject has a different way of thinking. The goal is not to “be a maths person” or “not a science person”. Learn the moves.

π

Patterns · logic · modelling

Mathematics

Numbers, algebra, geometry, statistics, functions and the occasional suspiciously specific train problem.

⚗

Observe · test · explain

Science

Biology, chemistry, physics, Earth science and the art of asking “okay, but why?” one more time.

⌛

People · power · place

Humanities

History, geography, economics, civics and all the evidence behind how humans made everything complicated.

Aa

Read · argue · create

English

Stories, persuasive writing, analysis, speaking, language and learning how to defend an interpretation.

⌘

Design · code · build

Digital & Technology

Coding, design thinking, data, systems, robotics and making computers do useful things on purpose.

✦

Imagine · make · communicate

Creative Arts

Visual art, media, drama, music and the slightly terrifying experience of showing other people your ideas.

Study studio

“Study harder” is terrible advice.

Choose what is actually going wrong. We will give you one useful next move.

01

Pick the closest problem

Your next move will appear here.

You do not need twelve productivity apps. You probably need one clear task.

Small challenge, big brain

Try something without being graded.

Learning becomes more interesting when every task is not attached to a percentage.

01

Explain it badly first

Choose a difficult concept and explain it in the simplest, messiest language you can. Then improve it.

02

Find the hidden assumption

Take a claim from science, history or media. What needs to be true for the claim to work?

03

Make the wrong answer

Create a believable incorrect answer and explain exactly why someone might choose it.

04

Connect two subjects

Can climate change become maths? Can poetry become history? Can art become technology?
