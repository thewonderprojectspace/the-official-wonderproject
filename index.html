01 / A NOTE BEFORE YOU ENTER
“You do not have to choose between being a mind full of questions and a human full of feeling.”

We are often asked to become one understandable thing. The smart one. The strong one. The lucky one. Follow the path with the least resistance. The person who is “fine.” But a life is never that tidy.

Here, curiosity is not a performance and growth is not a race. You may disagree without disrespect. You may begin messily. You may build several dreams at once. You may take longer than society finds convenient.

There is no hierarchy.
just two honest halves.

03 / THE SPACE BETWEEN

The Moon does not make its own light.
That does not make it less luminous.

It carries the Sun’s radiance through every phase—full, fractured, hidden, slowly building back. Perhaps we do too. The steady part of us does not disappear when the visible part is bruised. Sometimes it is simply shining from somewhere we cannot yet see.

Your phase is not your worth.
