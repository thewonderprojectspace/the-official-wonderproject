COURTYARD NOTE · 01

BEFORE SUBJECTS HAD LABELS

Learning was everywhere.

Maths lived in trade, measurement and architecture.

Science lived in fields, kitchens, bodies and stars.

Stories carried memory across generations.

Making taught the hands what words could not.

Learning never stopped being everywhere. We just got very good at putting it into boxes.
